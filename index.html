About Ever Latch

Jewelry that latches onto a memory.

Ever Latch was created to bring a modern, elevated permanent jewelry experience to Santa Barbara — feminine, effortless, and meaningful without feeling overdone. We offer custom-fit bracelets, anklets, necklaces, and charms in both sterling silver and 14k gold-filled materials, carefully selected for their quality, longevity, and everyday wearability.

Every piece is welded with intention using high-quality materials, personalized styling, and a one-on-one experience that feels both elevated and personal. Whether you're celebrating a milestone, creating a memory with friends, or simply investing in timeless jewelry you'll never want to take off, Ever Latch is designed to make the experience just as special as the piece itself.
